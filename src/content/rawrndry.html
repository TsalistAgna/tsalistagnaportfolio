Rawrndry

Project Overview

Rawrndry is a satisfying casual simulation game where you play as a dinosaur running a laundry service in the Stone Age. You wash, dry, and deliver clothes for cavemen to earn shiny rocks and grow your business.

I worked on the project as a UI/UX Designer and Project Manager. The experience centers on making the act of doing laundry enjoyable, rather than treating it as a chore to get through.

The Why

Can an ordinary chore become a game people want to play?

Laundry is normally boring and automated. In Rawrndry, a familiar task becomes a playful prehistoric simulation: you do the work by hand, step by step, as a dinosaur running a laundry service.

Replacing modern machines with manual interactions makes each task active and rewarding. Satisfying actions, visible progress, haptics, and humor are central to the concept. They give players reasons to enjoy a job they would normally avoid.

If players remember one thing, it should be the immersive interaction throughout the laundry process. Doing laundry can be fun, ridiculous, and satisfying.

The Experience

The game follows the laundry from a customer's drop-off through washing, drying, wrapping, and delivery. Each stage asks the player to take part instead of watching an automated process.

1

Wash

Move clothes from the dirty pile, scrub them, and place them in the clean pile. The washing screen turns sorting and cleaning into direct actions.

2

Dry

The drying scene uses a microphone prompt and a progress indicator. Players can see the laundry move toward completion as they interact.

3

Wrap

A directional drag prompt guides the wrapping action, turning clean clothes into a finished package.

4

Deliver

Drag the package to the customer. A day reward screen closes the loop by showing the clothes finished and the shiny rocks earned.

Design Focus

  • Hands-on play: Washing, drying, and wrapping are actions the player performs, so the ordinary task becomes the gameplay.
  • Visible progress: Progress feedback and a reward recap make each step and its result easy to understand.
  • Playful guidance: Contextual prompts explain what to do at the moment an interaction starts, while the dinosaur setting and humor keep the tone light.
  • Physical feel: Haptics are part of the intended satisfying interaction, alongside movement and feedback on screen.

Gameplay Screens

The screens show the game from the start menu through the laundry stages, instructions, delivery, and the daily reward.

Interface Elements

The interface uses textured panels, bold buttons, clear navigation controls, a day and currency display, and a reward summary to support the prehistoric visual style.
